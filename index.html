Master's Thesis • NISER Bhubaneswar

Venus Surface Compositional Analysis

Advisor: Dr. Guneshwar Thangjam

Conducted research at NISER Bhubaneswar focused on Venus surface compositional analysis using Venus Express (VIRTIS) data, Venus GRAM, and radiative transfer models. The research investigates atmospheric window radiance to isolate surface thermal emissions from dense atmospheric scattering and absorption.
